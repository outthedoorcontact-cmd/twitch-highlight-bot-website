Video clips are stored on your computer. TikTok account linking and publishing are planned features, not currently available.

How it works today

The Windows version is an early prototype. It uses audio activity to suggest highlight moments; it cannot guarantee every exciting moment will be found. The prototype has not been fully validated on a live stream. It does not currently upload videos to TikTok or connect to TikTok accounts.
