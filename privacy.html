Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current Twitch Highlight Bot desktop prototype handles data. Contact: outthedoorcontact@gmail.com.

This describes the current locally running prototype. TikTok sign-in and upload integrations are planned but not connected yet. This policy must be updated before launching those features.

1. Data handled by the current app

When you run the prototype and enter a Twitch channel URL, the app accesses that public livestream, records temporary segments for processing, analyzes audio activity to propose highlights, and exports video files to a local folder. It may also write local recording or error logs. This processing occurs on the computer running the app.

2. Where your clips go

The prototype saves edited clips in the local “Twitch Clips” folder and uses a temporary recording folder during processing. The present version is not designed to upload clips, Twitch credentials, or TikTok credentials to an Out The Door Media server. Clips are not automatically posted to TikTok.

3. TikTok connection (planned)

A future version may offer TikTok Login Kit authorization and the Content Posting API to transfer clips you approve. If introduced, the app would need to describe any TikTok profile information, access tokens, posting permissions, transmission, storage, retention, and revocation procedures before use. The current prototype does not have these features.

4. Sharing and outside services

Accessing a Twitch livestream involves contacting Twitch or its content-delivery providers, which have their own privacy practices. Visiting this publicly hosted website may involve hosting-provider technical logs under that provider's policies. We do not currently operate a separate analytics or advertising service within this static website.

5. Retention and deletion

You control video clips stored on your computer and may delete them using your file manager. Temporary recording segments are intended to be removed by the app after processing or when stopped normally, but may remain if the process crashes; users can delete any leftovers manually. Local recording logs may remain until deleted.

6. Security

Keep your computer secured and do not share passwords or secret keys. No security technology guarantees complete protection. The current version does not require your TikTok password.

7. Children and age requirements

Users must meet the age and account requirements of applicable third-party services. Anyone using the app should have authorization to record and post the footage involved.

8. Your choices and contact

You can stop running the app and delete local recordings at any time. For questions about this policy or data handled by Out The Door Media, email outthedoorcontact@gmail.com. We may update this policy as functionality changes, with the revision date shown above.
