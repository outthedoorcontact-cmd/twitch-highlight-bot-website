1. What the app does

The current Windows prototype records a Twitch stream entered by the user, identifies candidate highlights based on audio activity, converts those portions into vertical videos, and saves clips to the user's computer. Results may be inaccurate, incomplete, interrupted, or unavailable. An experimental TikTok Sandbox connection allows a user to authorize their account and upload a selected MP4 as a draft after confirmation. Automatic public publishing is not available.
